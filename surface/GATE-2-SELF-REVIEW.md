# Gate 2 — slop veto, run against my own draft before Oscar saw it

Screenshotted at 1440 and 390, opened, and looked at. Not reported off a file write.

## The signature device — ONE thing, from the subject

**Each prompt is set at its true length, with its outcome trail hanging beneath it.**
Operator a's prompt is one long specific line. Operator b's is two words with "never mind" under it.
**You see who prompts better by shape, before reading a word.**

Slop test — *if removing it loses no information, it is decoration.* Remove it and the comparison
becomes a table of two numbers. It survives. Not from a component library; it is the subject.

## Verdicts

### D3 · TRACK — **KILLED, and the reason is the project's own thesis**
I hardcoded the track widths at `34%` and `72%`. **There is no measured duration in the data.**
Then the closing copy says *"the shorter prompt cost twice the line"* — a claim derived from a
number I invented.

That is the **fake-precision-dressed-as-auditability** kill-list item, and it is fabricated evidence
inside a product whose entire pitch is that numbers come with their probe. Shipping it would hand a
judge the exact contradiction to point at.

*Salvageable only if the bar length is bound to something real — turn count is measurable. Not
worth the iteration when D2 is stronger. Ground is also near-black with a lone accent pair, which
sits close to a named AI cluster.*

### D1 · LEDGER — passes, with three defects visible only on screen
- **Half a screen of dead space below the fold at 1440×1150.** On an unedited take that is a long
  hold on nothing.
- The lede orphans *"cold."* onto its own line; `.sub` wraps two words early.
- **`ep. 1` is decoration carrying no data** — there is one episode, so the label says nothing.
  Gate 2 kill unless it shows a real count.

### D2 · MARGINALIA — **RECOMMENDED**
The margin notes do real analytical work rather than ornament:

| operator a | operator b |
|---|---|
| names the file · names the symbol · states the exit condition · asks to see it first | no file · no symbol · no exit condition · "never mind" |

**That column IS the argument.** It answers *why did this prompt win* without a sentence of
explanation, which is the design making the case rather than captioning it.

It also makes the thesis literal — *a prompt is writing, and someone in the building is better at
it* — and editorial serif is off-distribution for a developer tool, so it passes the stranger test:
**you could name this app from the screenshot.**

Deliberately dodged the banned warm-cream + serif + terracotta cluster: the ground is a cool
grey-blue, and **the loser carries no colour at all — its signal is absence**, not a red tint.

Defects to fix before filming: the closing file path is cramped inline; the abandoned block needs
more air beneath it.
